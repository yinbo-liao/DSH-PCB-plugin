# Non-goals - LDO3V3

- Not a battery charger, not a power-path manager.
- No USB-C receptacle, no PD negotiation (the input is a screw terminal).
- No enclosure, no mounting in a product; bench supply module only.
- No certification work (CE/FCC/UL). Compliance is declared "not assessed".
- No firmware - this board has no programmable device.
- No EMC pre-compliance measurement; the risk is accepted and recorded.
